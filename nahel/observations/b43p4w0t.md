Verify-by-driving is worthless if it runs before the review round's fixes. The first build discharged its drive obligation at 00:15 and then shipped epic-review fixes at 00:42 and 00:53 without re-driving; one of those fixes introduced the double-printed critical events that a player met on the very next real game. Two of the four defects found by driving on 2026-08-24 survived a 369/0 suite because every assertion was about what the code computes and none about how the played game reads.
